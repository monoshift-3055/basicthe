Developed by the Kenneth H. Cooper Institute, and as a part of the FitnessGram program, it’s not about competition—it’s about personal growth and promoting lifelong health.

The PACER Test is a multi-stage fitness test where participants run back and forth across a 20-meter space at a progressively faster pace, guided by audio cues.
